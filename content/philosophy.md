I have been thinking about various philosophical concepts and figures, some of which are related to mathematics.

1-In terms of philosophy of mathematics, I am quite close to Albert Lautman and his ideas of the mathematical real. I believe that an effective interpretation of recent MathxAI advances can be done via Lautmanian mathematics and Simondonian view of technical objects. If you are a mathematician/philosopher interested in hearing more, you are welcome to contact me.

2-In terms of more classical philosophy, I mostly think about Spinozist ontology, Deleuzo-Guattarian analysis of capitalism and desire, and Bergson's time.

3-I often combine my reading on Spinoza with the works of Nietzsche, mostly interpreted through Bataille, Klossowski and Deleuze.
